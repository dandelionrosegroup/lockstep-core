# Privacy Policy

**Lockstep Chain Protocol** — Last updated: 2026-03-10

Lockstep Chain Protocol is a fully local MCP server. All data is stored on your machine in the directory you configure during installation.

## Data Collection

**None.** Lockstep does not collect, transmit, or share any data.

## Network Access

**None.** Lockstep makes no network requests of any kind. It operates entirely on your local filesystem.

## Analytics and Telemetry

**None.** There is no usage tracking, crash reporting, or telemetry of any kind.

## Third-Party Sharing

**Never.** Your project data stays on your machine. Lockstep has no external dependencies that phone home, no cloud services, and no data pipelines.

## Data Storage

All data is stored as human-readable YAML files in the data directory you configure (default: `~/.lockstep/data/`). You own your data completely and can inspect, edit, move, or delete it at any time.

## Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, open an issue at [github.com/dandelionrosegroup/lockstep-core/issues](https://github.com/dandelionrosegroup/lockstep-core/issues).
